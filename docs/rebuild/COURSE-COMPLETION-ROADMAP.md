# Course Completion Roadmap

**Status:** canonical delivery order
**Updated:** 2026-08-01

## Sequence

```text
verified MTH1W course
  -> every remaining Grade 9 course
  -> Grade 10 courses
  -> Grade 11 courses
  -> Grade 12 courses
  -> optional adaptive sequencing and AI tutor enhancements
```

Work stays on the current course until its completion gate passes. The team
does not create a thin collection of partial courses and call that grade
coverage.

## Meaning of “course complete”

A course is complete only when a student can, without AI:

- navigate every unit and lesson;
- learn from reviewed instruction and worked examples;
- complete guided, independent, and mixed practice;
- take lesson checks, quizzes, unit assessments, and cumulative review;
- understand feedback and correct work;
- use accessible and printable/offline alternatives; and
- inspect governed completion evidence while an educator can review and
  correct it.

The course must also have a pinned official-source inventory, licensing
disposition, educator content review, automated verification, and accurate
public documentation. Record count, generated exercises, or passing software
tests alone do not satisfy this definition.

## Current implementation milestone

The app now exposes source-mapped Unit 1 as an offline draft preview with three
lessons, two or more reasoning routes per lesson, worked examples, 33 practice
items, delayed unit-quiz feedback, correction attempts, and a transparent
performance-task rubric. The full-course route remains closed because the
remaining eight units are specifications, not delivered lessons, and the
external educator and licensing gates have not passed.

## Grade 9 catalogue gate

The exact remaining Grade 9 course catalogue depends on jurisdiction, school
program, and learner pathway. It will be pinned from official sources before
implementation. No course is labelled supported merely because a preliminary
record exists in the current experimental Ontario corpus.
