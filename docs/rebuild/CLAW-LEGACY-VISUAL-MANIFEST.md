# Claw Academy legacy visual manifest

Status: recovered-source provenance registry. This file records legacy Claw visual evidence without making recovered material current product canon by survival alone.

## Why this exists

Legacy C.L.A.W. Academy material contains multiple design generations, misleading filenames, generated-sheet labeling errors, and text/image conflicts. Visual decisions therefore require provenance, comparison, and an explicit canon decision rather than first-image-wins inheritance.

A recovered asset may establish what a **legacy implementation** intended. It does not automatically become Claw v2 canon.

- `black cat` = preserved earlier legacy description;

- current Claw v2 canon = still requires explicit approval rather than automatic inheritance.

This is no longer an equal unresolved orange-vs-black fork.

## Ingestion rules for additional legacy art

For each recovered image, record:

1. stable reference ID;
2. source/date/context;
3. original filename or source locator;
4. exact digest where available;
5. dimensions/media type;
6. observable visual facts;
7. text/image/code conflicts;
8. whether identity is visible in the art, inferred from filename, or established by independent source;
9. canon status: `non-canonical`, `candidate`, `approved`, or `superseded`;
10. accessibility or child-safety concerns relevant to reuse.

Do not silently edit, recolour, crop, upscale, or regenerate a recovered source and then treat the derivative as the original. Derivatives need their own IDs and provenance.

## Canon-lock rule

A future visual/character bible should be locked only after:

- the recovered image/source set has been compared;
- major contradictions are explicitly resolved;
- chosen designs support current Claw architecture and accessibility requirements;
- generated-label/file-name errors are separated from reliable evidence;
- characters do not require real-person likeness data to function;
- the choice is explicitly approved and versioned.

This manifest records historical evidence. It does not silently turn legacy frequency into current canon.
