# Ontario Curriculum Demonstration Assurance

The current Ontario curriculum implementation is a **source-aligned demonstration** unless a later assurance record explicitly promotes a defined scope.

It is designed to align as closely as practicable with the identified published Ontario curriculum sources available for the capsule and to demonstrate how Axiom Education can map curriculum expectations into adaptive learning experiences.

It is **not**, by default:

- Ontario Ministry of Education approved;
- school-board approved;
- independently or qualified-human reviewed;
- accredited;
- an Ontario credit-bearing course;
- a substitute for an authorized school's own assessment, reporting, or credential-granting obligations.

## Why freeze a demonstration capsule

A demonstration capsule may be intentionally frozen once its source set, mappings, behavior, and assurance state are recorded. Freezing makes the demonstrated platform behavior reproducible instead of silently changing underneath a review or demo.

A later, better verified capsule should supersede the frozen capsule with new provenance rather than rewriting the historical assurance record.

## Assurance ladder

Axiom Education now distinguishes:

1. `unverified`
2. `source-aligned-demonstration`
3. `machine-audited`
4. `human-reviewed`
5. `institution-approved`
6. `jurisdiction-approved`
7. `accredited`

These levels are deliberately non-transitive. For example, machine auditing does not imply qualified human review, institution approval does not imply Ministry/jurisdiction approval, and jurisdiction approval does not automatically imply a credit-bearing accreditation claim.

## Required evidence

Any alignment claim requires identified source evidence. Human-reviewed or stronger assurance claims require review evidence for the declared scope. Accreditation is represented only when an applicable authorized body and evidence explicitly support the accreditation claim.

This keeps the platform free to demonstrate highly capable Ontario-aligned learning now without overstating what has actually been independently verified.
